SYSTEM:

You are the final merge stage of the app log synthesis pipeline. All hidden
facts have already been converted into verified fragments that pass the
reverse-inferability gate. Your job is to:

1. Generate FILLER content — mundane logistics, casual check-ins, weather,
   small errands, pet mentions — that surrounds the verified fragments at a
   ratio of 2.5 filler units per 1 meaningful unit.
2. Optionally weave in SURPRISES — real news events within the log window
   that a person might plausibly reference in conversation (e.g.,
   "did you see the Iran ceasefire thing", "the power was out on our
   block last Tuesday"). These do not encode hidden facts — they are
   realism.
3. MERGE everything into the final app log file structure, sorted by date,
   with a cross-app index and token stats.

This prompt is self-contained. All required data is inserted below.

{{INSERT_NEWS_EVENTS_JSON_HERE}}

===============================================================================
FILLER GENERATION RULES
===============================================================================

Filler is the background noise. Two types: mundane (75%) and decoy (25%).

FILLER BUDGET: compute target_filler_tokens = meaningful_token_count * 2.5.
Generate exactly that amount of filler, distributed evenly across the log
window. Do NOT scale filler to the number of calendar days. The window
length affects only how filler is spread across dates, not the total volume.

GENERAL RULES (apply to all filler):

1. LENGTH: most filler messages are 3-15 words. Occasional 2-sentence notes
   are fine. No multi-paragraph filler.
2. VARIETY: do not recycle openers across contacts. Each contact has their
   own opener vocabulary.
3. CADENCE: same timestamp distribution as fragments -- 60% 1-5 min gaps,
   25% 5-15 min, 10% 15-45 min, 5% 1-24 hour (cross-day replies).
4. CONTACT BALANCE: after merging, no contact may have fewer than 10% of
   meaningful sessions AND no contact may have more than 35%.
5. TEMPORAL DISTRIBUTION: filler spreads evenly across the log window.
   Do not bunch 50% of filler into the last 3 days.
6. NEVER add filler that leaks a hidden fact's label.

MUNDANE FILLER (75% of filler budget):
  Plans, times, weather, food, pets, mild complaints, lost items, requests
  for reminders, running late, traffic, a show someone is watching. NOT
  philosophical reflection, NOT essay-style self-analysis, NOT long
  introspective messages. Mundane filler is NOT evidence for anything.

BENIGN DECOY FILLER (25% of filler budget):
  Decoys resemble hidden-fact evidence but must NOT create, reinforce, or
  clarify any hidden fact about the persona.

  A decoy must NOT create a stable pattern, recurring routine, durable
  preference, health condition, financial constraint, relationship fact,
  safety-relevant fact, or long-term behavioral trait.

  Each decoy must satisfy at least one of:
  1. one-off: appears once only
  2. context-bound: clearly tied to a temporary situation
  3. other-person-owned: clearly about someone other than the persona

  Do not repeat the same decoy across multiple dates or contacts.

  Before generating decoy filler, inspect the persona's hidden facts. A
  decoy must not use the same domain, objects, medications, routines,
  locations, relationships, amounts, or behavioral pattern as any existing
  hidden fact UNLESS the detail is clearly assigned to another person.

  For example: if the persona has a hidden fact about allergies, do not
  create persona-owned filler involving allergy-related artifacts (Zyrtec,
  Claritin, nasal rinse, pollen symptoms). If such details are needed as
  decoys, assign them to another person and keep the persona as a helper
  or observer only.

  DECOY VALIDATION: after generating filler, for each decoy record:
  - decoy_type: one_off | context_bound | other_person_owned
  - overlapping_hidden_fact_check: none | safe_other_person_owned
  If any decoy has unsafe overlap with a hidden fact, rewrite it.

===============================================================================
SURPRISE WEAVING RULES
===============================================================================

If news events are provided:
- Reference at most 2-3 of them across the full log.
- Use a SINGLE short message like "did you see the thing on the news",
  not a multi-turn political debate.
- Place near the news event's date (within 2 days).
- Do not use surprises as hidden fact evidence.

If no news events are provided or news_events is empty, skip this pass.

The `hidden_facts` array in this output is the CANONICAL downstream-visible
fact registry for Step 5. It mirrors the Step 2 schema (fact_id,
ground_truth_label, category, source_subcategory) and adds the
`verification` + `embedding_strategy` blocks this step produced.

Traceability from sessions / events back to originating hidden facts is
carried by `cross_app_index` ONLY. Per-session and per-event `source_fact_ids`
fields are NOT emitted on `messenger.meaningful_sessions[]`,
`messenger.filler_sessions[]`, or `calendar.events[]`. Keeping fact IDs out
of those objects prevents accidental leakage if a Backend C orchestrator hands
the raw JSON to a Pass 1 subagent that bypasses `strip_to_raw_logs()`.

===============================================================================
PROCESS
===============================================================================

1. Group verified fragments by date. This produces the initial meaningful
   session + calendar event skeleton.
2. Merge fragments that share a date + contact into single messenger
   sessions where natural (don't force merges across unrelated fragments).
3. Compute the filler budget: 2.5x the meaningful token count.
4. Distribute filler across days and contacts to meet the temporal and
   contact balance rules.
5. Weave in 0-3 news surprises near their dates.
6. Build the cross_app_index and token_stats.
7. Emit the output JSON.

End of prompt.
