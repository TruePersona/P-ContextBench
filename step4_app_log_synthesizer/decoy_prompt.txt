CORE RULE
===============================================================================
A decoy may distract the model, but it must not actually support, reinforce,
clarify, contradict, or overlap unsafely with any hidden fact about the persona.
A decoy must not create:
- a stable pattern
- recurring routine
- durable preference
- health condition
- medication routine
- financial constraint
- relationship fact
- safety-relevant fact
- legal or risk-relevant fact
- long-term behavioral trait
===============================================================================
VALID DECOY TYPES
===============================================================================
Each decoy must satisfy at least one of these types:
1. one_off:
   The decoy appears once only and is not repeated across dates, contacts, apps,
   or calendar events.
2. context_bound:
   The decoy is clearly tied to a temporary situation and does not generalize
   into a stable persona trait.
3. other_person_owned:
   The decoy detail clearly belongs to someone other than the persona.
   The persona may be a helper, observer, or logistical participant only.
Prefer one_off or context_bound decoys from domains unrelated to the hidden facts.
Use other_person_owned only when the decoy touches a domain similar to a hidden fact.
===============================================================================
CONTACT FLEXIBILITY RULE FOR MESSENGER DECOYS
===============================================================================
Messenger decoys may be either contact-specific or contact-flexible.
Use contact_policy = "specific" when the decoy only makes sense with one particular contact from CORRECTED SOCIAL CIRCLE.
  - set preferred_contact to a real contact from CORRECTED SOCIAL CIRCLE;
  - use sender = "{{PERSONA_NAME}}" when the persona is speaking;
  - use the real contact name when the other conversation participant is speaking;
Examples:
- a decoy about Mom's porch should use Mom;
- a decoy about a coworker's meeting should use that coworker;
- a decoy about a friend's child should use that friend.
Use contact_policy = "flexible" when the decoy could naturally fit into a conversation with many different existing contacts.
Flexible decoys should look like high-salience false leads, not ordinary mundane filler. They may involve a one-time document, borrowed item, temporary appointment, misplaced object, isolated misunderstanding, someone else's issue, or a bounded legal/financial/health/work-looking artifact.
However, they must remain non-evidence. Each flexible decoy must be clearly bounded by one-off context, other-person ownership, or explicit denial that the detail belongs to the persona.
Example 1 - finance-looking but one-off/context-bound:
- contact: "That overdue notice looked scary."
- {{PERSONA_NAME}}: "It was for the building HOA, not me personally."
- contact: "So not your debt?"
- {{PERSONA_NAME}}: "No, I just forwarded it to the board thread."
Example 2 - employment/legal-looking but not durable:
- contact: "That termination packet sounds serious."
- {{PERSONA_NAME}}: "It is not about me."
- contact: "Then why do you have it?"
- {{PERSONA_NAME}}: "I am proofreading the template before HR sends it."
For flexible-contact decoys:
- set preferred_contact = null;
- do not use contact names, nicknames, relationship-specific details, or private history;
- use sender = "{{PERSONA_NAME}}" when the persona is speaking;
- use sender = "contact" when the other conversation participant is speaking;
- the merge step will choose the actual contact and replace "contact" with that contact's name.
Do not invent new contacts.
===============================================================================
FLEXIBILITY RULE FOR CALENDAR DECOYS
===============================================================================
Calendar decoys are floating candidates. The merge step assigns their final date and time.
For calendar decoys:
- do not assign a fixed date;
- do not assign fixed start_time or end_time;
- include only the event title, location, participants, and notes;
- include participants only if the decoy is impossible without them;
- do not include placeholder participants such as "contact" or "other person name";
- if a real participant is required, use an existing contact from CORRECTED SOCIAL CIRCLE;
- if no participant is required, set participants = [];
- clearly temporary or one-off;
- designed to be scheduled by the merge step.
===============================================================================
PLACEMENT HINT RULE
===============================================================================
placement_hint tells the merge step where this decoy is best placed.
Allowed placement_hint values:
- same_contact_meaningful_session:
  Prefer inserting this messenger decoy into an existing meaningful messenger session with the selected contact, if it fits naturally and remains non-evidence.
- separate_filler_session:
  Prefer placing this messenger decoy as a separate filler session.
- flexible:
  The merge step may choose either insertion into a meaningful session or a separate filler session, depending on what fits naturally.
placement_hint is guidance only. The merge step may override it if needed for safety, traceability, or naturalness.
===============================================================================
HIDDEN-FACT OVERLAP RULE
===============================================================================
Before generating a decoy, inspect the selected hidden facts.
A decoy must not use the same domain, objects, medications, routines, locations,
relationships, amounts, or behavioral pattern as any existing hidden fact unless
the detail is explicitly owned by another person and cannot reasonably be
attributed to the persona.
Allowed overlap_check values during candidate evaluation:
- none:
The decoy does not overlap with any selected hidden fact.
- safe_other_person_owned:
The decoy touches a hidden-fact-like domain, but the relevant detail clearly
belongs to someone other than the persona and cannot reasonably be attributed
to the persona.
- unsafe_overlap:
The decoy creates, reinforces, clarifies, contradicts, or overlaps with a
hidden fact in a way that could reasonably be attributed to the persona.
Final output rule: Do not output unsafe decoys.
For each decoy, explicitly compare it against every hidden fact before setting overlap_check.
If a candidate would overlap unsafely with a hidden fact, rewrite it as a safe
unrelated-domain decoy, make it clearly other-person-owned, or omit it.
If fewer safe decoys are available than target_decoy_count decoys,
output fewer rather than weakening the safety rules.
If the persona has a hidden fact about allergies, do NOT create persona-owned
filler involving allergy-related artifacts such as:
- Zyrtec
- Claritin
- Allegra
- nasal rinse
- pollen symptoms
- itchy eyes
- antihistamines
- allergy appointments
If such details are used, they must be explicitly owned by another person.
Examples:
Unsafe:
  Persona: "I picked up Claritin again before the pollen gets bad."
Safe other-person-owned:
  Contact: "Can we stop at CVS? Luca forgot his Claritin."
  Persona: "Sure, I need paper towels anyway."
Safer unrelated-domain decoy:
  Persona: "I stopped at CVS for paper towels and printer tape."
===============================================================================
DECOY DESIGN RULES
===============================================================================
Allowed decoy surfaces:
- one-time errand
- temporary schedule disruption
- someone else's health, finance, logistics, school, or family issue
- one-off purchase
- one-off meal/place mention
- temporary work complication
- one-time media/article recommendation
- casual request that does not become a pattern
Avoid:
- repeated behaviors
- repeated objects
- repeated locations if location preference is a hidden fact
- repeated medication or health references
- persona-owned financial amounts if financial facts exist
- anything that could become source evidence for a test case
Messenger decoys:
- usually 2-4 messages
Calendar decoys:
- one event only;
Deduplication:
Do not output duplicate or near-duplicate decoys across dates, contacts, messenger, or calendar. If two decoys share the same idea, object, domain, behavioral pattern, wording, or apparent implication, keep only the safer and more context-bound one; rewrite or omit the other.
Do not assign final dates or times.
LOG WINDOW is provided only for context.
The merge step will distribute selected decoys across the log window.
Use CONTACT USAGE COUNTS only when choosing a specific contact is necessary.
For flexible-contact decoys, leave final contact choice to the merge step.
